## Section: Partner / corporate callback form (variant with selector)

This is a separate inline form from the standard Callback lightbox — same fields, plus one
extra required field at the top: **Ich bin: / I am:** `Vermittler` / `Firmenkunde` (`Referral
partner` / `Corporate client`).

- Headline: `Partner werden`
